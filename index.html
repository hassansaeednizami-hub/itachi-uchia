ITACHI UCHIHA

The Legendary Shinobi

Itachi Uchiha was one of the strongest members of the Uchiha Clan and the mysterious Akatsuki. He sacrificed everything for peace and his little brother Sasuke.

👁️ Sharingan

The Sharingan grants incredible visual abilities, prediction of enemy movements, and powerful Genjutsu.

Itachi awakened the Mangekyou Sharingan and gained abilities like Tsukuyomi, Amaterasu and Susanoo.

☁️ Akatsuki

As a member of Akatsuki, Itachi secretly protected the Hidden Leaf while carrying out dangerous missions.
